# Authorship (local)

Copy to `authorship.md` (gitignored) and fill in. The skill reads it before writing.

For each text you produce, name who holds the pen: `ai`, `draft` (AI drafts, owner edits), `check` (owner writes, AI checks), `me` (owner writes), and whether the owner may dictate it.

| Text | Pen | Dictate? | Language |
|---|---|---|---|
| Company website | draft | yes | en, fi |
| Personal website | check | yes | en |
| PR descriptions | ai | no | en |
